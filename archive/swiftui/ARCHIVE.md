# SwiftUI archive

Frozen copy of the native macOS SwiftUI/AppKit Grabbit app, kept for reference while the product is remade in Electron.

- **Git branch:** `archive/swiftui`
- **Git tag:** `swiftui-archive`
- **Restore full tree:** `git checkout archive/swiftui`

Do not build from this folder as the primary app — the Electron app on `main` is the product going forward.
